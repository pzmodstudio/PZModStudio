# What's new

Your Workshop mods that were updated since you last played, and the ones whose update is still waiting in Steam, each with its change notes. Read it before you load a save in progress, so an update that breaks saves does not catch you by surprise.

## What you see

- At the top, three tabs pick the time span:
  - **Since you last played (date)**: the date is the latest session the Studio Link mod recorded, or the last time Steam ran the game, whichever is newer.
  - **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days**.
- **Only mods on in my list**: ticked by default. Untick it to also see updated mods that are installed but switched off.
- A count of mods.
- The round arrow at the top right reads everything again.
- One card per Workshop item.

## Reading a card

- **Updated (date)**: the version on your disk was published after the start of the time span.
- **Update waiting in Steam**: Steam knows a newer version it has not downloaded yet.
- One chip per profile that has one of its mods on, with the active profile's in green (the list the game reads). **On in no profile** when none has it on.
- Under the tags, the change notes of each update, newest first, with their date.
- **All notes on the Workshop** opens the item's full change notes page in your web browser.

## Heading off an update

When a card says **Update waiting in Steam**, you can keep the version you have before Steam replaces it:

1. Press **Keep the version I have**. The Studio copies each of its mods as they are now.
2. The button turns into **Version kept**.
3. If the update later breaks your save, open the Mod List, right-click the mod and choose **Kept versions…** to put the old one back.

Do this before Steam downloads the update: once it does, the old files are gone.

## Change notes

- The list appears first without notes (read from your PC only), then the notes are read from Steam.
- **Reading the change notes...** shows while that happens.
- To spare Steam, only a few pages are read at a time. Cards not read yet say so, and a **Read the notes of N more** button reads the next ones.
- If Steam is limiting requests, a card says to try again in a few minutes.
- Notes already read are kept on your PC and asked for again only when Steam has a newer version.
- **The author wrote no notes for these updates** means the change notes page was empty.

## The mod's own changelog

Many mods ship a changelog in their own folder (`ChangeLog.txt`, `CHANGELOG.md`). The Studio reads it from disk, so it works offline and for mods that are not on the Workshop.

- A card shows the entries you have not read yet, under **From the mod's own changelog**, next to the Workshop notes.
- **read in the game**: the game's mod alerts already showed you the newest entry, so it is not new here either.
- Mods in `Zomboid/mods` (local, or from Nexus) that are on in your list appear here when their changelog gets a new entry, marked **local mod**. **Mark as read** takes the card away until the next entry. The first time the Studio sees a mod's changelog it only remembers it, without showing it.
- **Support the author**: the links the author put in the changelog (Ko-fi, Patreon, GitHub, Discord...) and the support links of its Workshop description (Ko-fi, Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal, GitHub Sponsors, Liberapay, Boosty, Open Collective), opened in your browser.

## Good to know

- Mods you installed by hand have no Workshop history: they appear here only through their own changelog.
- The sidebar badge counts the mods of your active list that were updated since your last session or are waiting in Steam.
- "Since you last played" is only as good as the sessions the Studio knows of: play with the Studio Link mod on so it records them.
