PZ Mod Index

What the community knows about Project Zomboid mods: the builds they run on, what they clash with, their known issues and the fixes PZ Mod Studio applies. Every line says the build it was seen on and whether the Studio verified it or a person reported it. The Studio downloads this same data once a day.

Load-order rules and incompatible pairs

From community/rules.json: what mod.info does not say. The Studio's Auto-Sort follows the rules and Mod Health warns when both of a pair are on.

Players' lists

Mod lists other players shared, each read by a person before it is published. In PZ Mod Studio: Browse → My lists → From the community copies one into your lists (nothing is installed until you press Subscribe to all missing), and Share with the community on one of yours proposes it here. Without the Studio, tell me on the itch.io page and I will add it.

Add what you know

Know that a mod clashes with another, or runs on a build the index does not say? Tell me on the itch.io page: write the mod, the build and what happened, and a person adds it. From the Studio: right-click a mod → Share what the Studio saw of this mod… prepares the text for you.
