01 · Auto-Sort

One button.
The game's own rules.

The game reads mod.info: what a mod requires, what it loads after and before. Auto-Sort follows exactly that, and leaves the rest where you put it.

  • Each mod after what it needs and after the mods its author says it loads after.
  • Nothing else moves. Your order is your order; the previous one is kept for Undo.
  • Mod Health catches what sorting cannot fix: a mod your build can't load, a requirement missing or turned off, two mods that don't go together.

02 · Two halves

The app on your PC.
The Link in your game.

The Studio does the thinking on Windows. The Link, a small Lua mod on the Workshop, puts it where you actually play: the main menu, the Mods screen, the moment before a save loads.

Windows · portable app

PZ Mod Studio

  • Profiles: a light setup, a heavy one and a co-op one, side by side. Switching unsubscribes nothing.
  • Load order, Mod Health, crash cards from console.txt, each error with the mod behind it.
  • Mod Merger: two mods that replace one file, merged into a package that loads last. The mods stay untouched.
profile · health · saves
In the game · Steam Workshop

The Link mod

  • The active profile on the Mods screen. What you change in the game goes back to the profile.
  • Mod Health marks on the mods with a problem, and your own notes on each mod.
  • A card on the main menu: the profile, the problems, what changed in the save Continue would load.

The Link, even without the app

  • An error notice that names the mod whose Lua failed, with a report ready for its author.
  • A clearer Mods screen: search, filters, a mod comes on with what it needs, a load order you can drag.

Safe by design

  • Only Lua: five plain-text files. No Java, no DLLs, nothing compiled.
  • It can't start programs or open pages, and it sends nothing anywhere.

03 · When something breaks

From the crash
to the fix.

The game's log says something failed. The Studio says which mod, what it means, and what to do about it.

1

The game names the mod

The moment a mod's Lua fails, the Link says which one, and the one that called it. Copy report puts it on the clipboard for the author.

2

The app explains it

Monitor reads console.txt and turns each error into a card: the mod behind it, what it means, whether the game carried on.

3

One click fixes it

Mod Health checks the list the way the game does and, when there is a fix, offers it: turn on the requirement, move the mod, use a compatible copy.

04 · Saves

A check before
every save loads.

Loading a save with different mods can lose items, vehicles or whole map areas for good. So when a save's mods changed since you last played it, you see what changed first.

  • Automatic zip backups when you leave a save and before it loads with changed mods. Each one is read back after it's written.
  • What is inside: each character, where they are and what they carry; each vehicle and what is in it.
  • Map mods a save doesn't load found and added back, so no bare land where a map should be.
