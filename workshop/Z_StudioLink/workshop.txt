description=[h1]Know which mod broke your game, and fix it.[/h1]
description=When a mod fails, the game prints a stack trace and moves on. The Link names the mod, says where it failed and keeps every error. With the free PZ Mod Studio app it goes further: the fix in one click, a backup before a save loads with changed mods, and a way back when a Steam update breaks a mod.
description=
description=[b]On its own, the Link gives you:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]An error notice that names the mod[/b]: which mod failed, which one called it, where. Copy report puts it on the clipboard for the mod's author; every error of the session in one list.
description=[*][b]Errors kept on disk[/b], so a crash or a disconnect does not lose them.
description=[*][b]A clearer Mods screen[/b]: search, filters, a mod comes on with what it requires, a load order you can drag, undo.
description=[*][b]Shared files[/b]: when two of your mods ship the same Lua file, it marks which one wins.
description=[*][b]Reload mods[/b]: a mod you subscribe to while the game runs shows up without restarting.
description=[*][b]Server lines[/b]: your mods as a dedicated server's Mods=, WorkshopItems= and Map= lines, ready to paste.
description=[*][b]In your language[/b]: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Русский and 简体中文.
description=[*][b]For mod authors[/b]: PZModStudioLink.registerReport("YourModId", fn) adds your mod's debug info to the reports players copy for you.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]PZ Mod Studio[/b] is the app behind it: free and open source, for [b]Windows and Linux[/b], in the same seven languages. Profiles, load order, error diagnosis, conflicts, save backups, earlier versions of your mods and dedicated servers.

description=[list]
description=[*][b]The Doctor[/b]: ask "why did my game close?" in your language. It answers from your own sessions, with the evidence and one thing to do. No AI, nothing sent.
description=[*][b]Earlier versions kept[/b]: when a Steam update breaks a mod, go back to the version you played with.
description=[*][b]Simple view[/b] for new players: Play, your mods, and only what needs fixing.
description=[*][b]Servers[/b]: install the official server, restarts on a schedule, after a crash or a mod update, a Discord bot.
description=[*][b]Also[/b]: what changed in your updated mods, each author's support links, the Workshop inside the app, map mods on the real map, live reload for modders, an MCP server for AI assistants.
description=[/list]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pzmodstudio/PZModStudio/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/banners/s2-game.png[/img]
description=[h2]With the app open[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]The active profile on the Mods screen[/b]: what you turn on or off goes back to it.
description=[*][b]Diagnostics marks[/b] on the mods with a problem, and your notes on each mod.
description=[*][b]A card on the main menu[/b]: the profile, the problems found, whether your last games went badly.
description=[*][b]A live overlay[/b]: FPS, memory and the mods whose Lua takes the most time.
description=[*][b]Mark this moment[/b] on the pause menu: the Doctor then tells you what the log wrote in that minute.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]A check before every save loads[/h2]
description=Loading a save with different mods can lose items, vehicles or map areas for good. When a save's mods changed, the Link shows what changed and you choose: [b]Load it[/b] (backed up first), [b]Play on a copy[/b], or [b]Back[/b].
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pzmodstudio/PZModStudio/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/banners/s3-safe.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]The mod is only Lua you can read: no Java, no DLLs. It writes only its own small files and your mod list, and sends nothing anywhere.
description=[*]The app is open source (MIT): no account, no telemetry, no ads. It never edits Workshop mods, and changes a save only when you say yes, after a backup.
description=[*]Windows may show a SmartScreen notice the first time, only because the program has no paid code-signing certificate.
description=[/list]
